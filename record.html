Lisan ud Dawat Voice Recorder

  1. Sit in a quiet room. Turn off fans if you can.
  2. Hold the phone about a hand's length from your mouth.
  3. Read naturally, at your normal speed. Small mistakes are fine; re-record if you stumble a lot.
