What's happening (diagnostic log):

Works in Google Chrome, with internet on. Your voice is sent to Google's speech service to be converted into text.

Experiment mode

Type a Lisan ud Dawat sentence correctly, then speak it in each mode. The page scores how many words each mode got right.
